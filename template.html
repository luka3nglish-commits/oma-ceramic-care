The first stretch

Give it
time to bond

The coating keeps hardening after we hand the car back. Treat it gently early and it locks in for the long run.

The first 48 hours

Curing · keep it dry

0%Curing
  • ×
    Do not wash it.

    No water, no wiping, not even to knock dust off. The coating is still setting.

  • ×
    First 24 hours, keep it completely dry.

    No rain, no sprinklers, no sitting water while it is green. Park undercover if you can.

  • ✓
    After the first day, light rain is fine.

    Just do not let water pool and dry on it. If it does get wet, gently pat it dry with a clean soft towel. Do not rub.

  • ✓
    Bird mess or sap is the one exception.

    If something lands on it, gently flood it with water or lay a damp cloth over it with no pressure, then pat dry. Do not scrub. Or just call us.

  • ✓
    Otherwise just drive it.

    Driving is completely fine. It is standing water drying on the paint that you want to avoid.

The first week

Settling in

  • ×
    Hold off on washing for 7 days.

    Give the coating a full week before its first proper wash so it can finish hardening.

  • ×
    Nothing harsh on the paint.

    No degreasers, no traffic film removers, no waxes or polishes. The coating is the protection now.

  • ✓
    Rinse off bird mess and bugs straight away.

    A gentle hose rinse is fine if something lands on it. Do not scrub. More on that below.

  • ✓
    Watch it bead.

    Next time it rains, notice how the water balls up and runs off. That is the coating working.

From then on

The easy part

  • ×
    Never use automatic or brush car washes.

    The brushes swirl the coating and the harsh sprays strip it. Hand wash only. A touchless wash is okay in a pinch.

  • ✓
    Wash every couple of weeks.

    A coated car stays cleaner for longer and washes off far easier. A fortnightly wash keeps it looking its best.

  • ✓
    Use the wash routine below.

    pH neutral shampoo, two buckets, soft mitt, dry it off. That is the whole trick.

  • ✓
    Book a maintenance wash with OMA.

    Every few months we can refresh the coating and keep the beading strong. Details at the bottom.

Wash day

How to wash
a coated car

Tap each step. The order matters more than the effort. This is gentle, quick, and keeps the surface flawless.

Golden rule. Always wash in the shade, on a cool panel, never in direct sun. In the Queensland heat a sunny panel flash dries the water and leaves spots.
1 Rinse before you touch it +
Blast the car with a hose or pressure washer first. This floats off grit and dust so you are not dragging it across the paint. Never wipe a dry, dusty coated car.
2 Two buckets, pH neutral shampoo +
One bucket of suds with a pH neutral, ceramic safe car shampoo, one bucket of clean rinse water. Dunk and rinse your mitt in the clean bucket between panels so dirt never goes back on the car. A grit guard in each bucket helps, and keep a separate mitt for the lower doors and wheels since they carry the most grit.
3 Soft mitt, straight lines +
Use a clean, soft microfibre wash mitt. Wash top to bottom in straight lines, not circles. Light pressure only. The coating does the work, you are just lifting dirt.
4 Rinse it all off +
Rinse every panel thoroughly so no soap dries on the surface. Watch the water sheet straight off. A coated car rinses cleaner than you are used to.
5 Dry it, do not let it air dry +
Dry with a clean soft microfibre towel or a blower. Letting it air dry is the number one cause of water spots. To avoid fine swirls, do not drag the towel across dry paint. Blow the water off first, or mist a drying aid for slip, then blot and pat rather than wipe.
Optional boost. A spray sealant topper brings the beading right back, but not in the first 7 days. Apply it thin, in the shade on a cool panel, follow the directions on the bottle, and buff off every bit of residue with a clean dry cloth or it can streak.

If this happens

Don't stress

Bird droppings or tree sap

These are acidic and can etch even coated paint if they bake in the sun. Rinse or gently flush with water as soon as you can. Do not scrub it dry.
